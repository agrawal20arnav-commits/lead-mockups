Our Story: Where Culinary Roots Meet Modern Branches

Ananda Tree House was founded by a Nepali chef who returned from culinary school in Europe, bringing a global perspective to local traditions. His inspiration? A cherished childhood memory of his grandmother’s treehouse kitchen, where the scent of simmering lentils mingled with the whisper of leaves.

We've created a unique space where the interior blends bamboo lattice with Himalayan stone, forming a canopy of green that filters the bustling city noise. Our small courtyard of native orchids and hanging herbs provides a fragrant backdrop, while subtle lanterns cast a warm, earthy glow. Live acoustic performances by local musicians play softly, inviting diners to linger and savor both food and sound.

We aim to share the secret of blending local ingredients with global techniques, turning every bite into a story.
