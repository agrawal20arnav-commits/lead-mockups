About Butter Mellow

Butter Mellow was founded by a former pastry chef who returned to Pokhara after years in Kathmandu, driven by a vision to create a quiet retreat for locals and visitors alike. Our studio is a testament to the art of baking, where traditional techniques meet refined flavors.

Our interior features floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking Pokhara's serene lakeside, inviting natural light and calming views. Natural wood furnishings and subdued lighting create an atmosphere of tranquility, perfect for a moment of quiet indulgence. The comforting scent of fresh butter and baked goods fills the air, promising an experience of simple elegance.

We believe in the power of quality ingredients, which is why we meticulously source the finest local butter and other components. Every item on our menu is a handcrafted expression of our commitment to exceptional taste and a relaxed dining experience.
