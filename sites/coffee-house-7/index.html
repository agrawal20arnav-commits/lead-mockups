Our Story: A Revival of Heritage

Founded in 2017 by Maya Gurung, Coffee House is more than just a café; it's a re-imagined piece of history. Maya, a Nepali expatriate, returned to Kathmandu with a vision: to revive her family’s 19th-century teahouse.

She meticulously salvaged the original hand-turned wooden espresso machine, a testament to generations of craftsmanship, and transformed the space into a modern hub for coffee lovers. Our café bridges the past and present, offering a unique blend of tradition and contemporary comfort.

Our Ambiance

Step into a space where Kathmandu’s storied temples meet Scandinavian design. Carved wooden panels adorn the walls, complementing clean lines and a central stone hearth that crackles softly. A living wall of vibrant ferns and orchids purifies the air, providing a serene green backdrop for quiet reflection or collaborative work. It's an inspiring study spot that whispers Himalayan heritage.
