Our Story & Ambiance

Where High-Altitude Flavors Meet World-Class Coffee

The idea for Flavour Camp sparked during a 2015 trek to Annapurna when our founders, a Nepali chef and an Italian coffee roaster, swapped recipes over a steaming pot of masala tea. Their collaboration birthed Flavour Camp, a place where Nepal’s high‑altitude flavors meet world‑class coffee.

The restaurant opens onto a wooden terrace overlooking the lively Thamel market, with hand‑carved brass lanterns suspended from the rafters, while the scent of fresh cardamom and roasted local coffee drifts in. Inside, a living wall of ferns and a hand‑painted mural of Kathmandu’s rooftops create a tranquil urban oasis, punctuated by low‑profile wooden tables that invite intimate conversation.

Our Unique Camp Offerings:

  • On‑site micro‑roasting of Nepali coffee beans harvested at 6000m altitude.
  • Monthly workshops led by a renowned Nepali chef, teaching traditional techniques with a modern twist.
  • Rooftop herb garden that supplies fresh herbs to every dish.
