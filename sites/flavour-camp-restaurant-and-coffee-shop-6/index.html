About Flavour Camp

Flavour Camp is more than just a restaurant and coffee shop; it's a destination where culinary passion meets a welcoming atmosphere. Located in the vibrant heart of Thamel, Kathmandu, we are dedicated to providing an unforgettable experience for every guest.
