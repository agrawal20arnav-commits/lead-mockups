Our Story: From Rickshaw to Cafe

Petals Cafe began with founder Laxmi Devi, a former tea-room owner in Pokhara. Inspired by the vibrant colors of the Thamel market after a pilgrimage to Lumbini, she repurposed a decommissioned rickshaw into a mobile café. Her passion for local flavors and community eventually led her to settle in the historic Keshar Mahal, transforming her dream into the beloved Petals Cafe we know today.

We are more than just a cafe; we're a cultural experience, serving young tech workers, international tourists, and eco-conscious foodies alike.

Experience Our Unique Ambiance

The café sits on a terraced balcony overlooking the Thamel market, with hand-carved wooden tables painted in hand-stenciled Madhubani patterns. Soft Himalayan prayer flags flutter above, and a local guitarist plays sitar-infused folk tunes during brunch hours. The aroma of freshly roasted coffee beans mingles with cardamom, inviting guests to pause amid the city buzz.
