Our Story, Your Experience

Cafe Kefi was founded by Chef Sita Gurung, who returned to Kathmandu after a decade honing her craft in France. Inspired by the serene 7th-century Kefi monastery murals, she envisioned a place where traditional Nepali spices could dance with refined European café techniques.

Our café sits on a terraced balcony overlooking the Kathmandu Valley, offering a breathtaking vista. Stone walls carved with intricate mandala patterns and a vibrant living wall of native orchids create a serene atmosphere. Here, soft Himalayan flute music gently intermixes with the distant chatter of street vendors, crafting a truly unique soundscape.

Natural light floods our space through stunning stained-glass panels depicting local folklore, casting a warm, contemplative glow. We are a haven for young professionals seeking a productive yet culturally rich workspace, students craving authentic Nepali flavors, and international tourists eager for a taste of Kathmandu’s culinary heritage.

Our Unique Selling Points:

  • Handcrafted Beverages: Using locally sourced spices and teas collected from the hills of Gorkha and Lamjung.
  • On-Site Roasted Coffee: Coffee beans roasted by a Nepalese artisan, ensuring a fresh, single-origin experience.
  • Interactive AR Menu: Scan our menu with your smartphone to reveal the origin story of each ingredient.
