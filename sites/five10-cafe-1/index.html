Our Story & Ambiance

Founded in 2020 by siblings who trekked the breathtaking Langtang Valley, Five10 Cafe was born from a dream: to fuse Kathmandu's vibrant street food energy with a relaxed, inspiring vibe. They brought back a secret Lhasa chili recipe and a vision of a community hub.

More than just a cafe, Five10 is a creative workspace for Tribhuvan University students, a cultural haven for digital nomads, and a culinary adventure for Nepali food bloggers. We celebrate local artistry and flavors.

Unique Interior

Exposed terracotta brick walls, hand-woven Nepali textiles, and a living wall of Himalayan herbs create a warm, inviting space.

Acoustic Nights

Our corner stage hosts nightly acoustic performances, filling the air with local melodies and spoken word.

Aromatic Bliss

The comforting scent of freshly ground beans and steaming momos constantly fills the air, inviting you to linger.
