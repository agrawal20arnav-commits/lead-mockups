Our Story: A Rooftop Oasis

From Humble Beginnings to Thamel's Secret Garden

Founded in 2018 by street-food veteran Maya Sharma, Busy Bee Cafe Ktm grew from her roadside stall that sold ‘Lalita’s Pashmal’ and a humble pot of tea. Inspired by the city’s buzzing energy, she transformed her stall into a rooftop cafe that preserves the flavors of her childhood while embracing modern coffee culture.

Perched on a rooftop terrace overlooking the Thamel skyline, our cafe blends bamboo furnishings with hand-painted terracotta tiles, creating a breezy, sunlit oasis that feels like a secret garden. We invite young professionals, digital nomads, students, and eco-conscious locals to discover innovative Nepali-Western fusion in a vibrant, community-focused setting.

Enjoy live acoustic evenings and a rotating local art display that turn each visit into a cultural experience. Our small open kitchen showcases fresh, local ingredients being turned into fusion delights right before your eyes.
