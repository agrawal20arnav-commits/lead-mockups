Our Story

A Courtyard Reborn: The Genesis of The Third Space

The Third Space Cafe was born from a serendipitous discovery when three friends stumbled upon a forgotten courtyard nestled behind a heritage building in Kathmandu. Its ancient stone walls, echoing centuries of history, became the canvas for a vision: a living space that not only preserves the past but also embraces modern culinary fusion.

We transformed this hidden gem into a vibrant hub where the city's rich heritage meets contemporary Nepali flavors. Our commitment to local artistry is reflected in our handcrafted pottery mugs, each telling the story of its maker, and our in-house rooftop garden, supplying fresh native orchids and herbs to our kitchen.

Join us for live acoustic evenings featuring Nepali folk jazz fusion, where local musicians find their rhythm and our community finds its beat. The Third Space Cafe is more than just a cafe; it's a celebration of Kathmandu's spirit, a place to connect, create, and savor.
